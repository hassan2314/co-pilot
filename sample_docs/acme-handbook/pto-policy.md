# Time Off Policy

This policy covers paid time off, sick leave, company holidays, and parental leave for full-time ACME employees. Contractors follow the terms in their agreement. Questions go to hr@acme.example or the HR portal.

## Paid time off
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off (PTO) each calendar year. PTO accrues on January 1 and can be used for vacation, personal errands, or mental-health days. Part-time employees accrue PTO in proportion to scheduled hours.

Submit requests in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for absences of three or more consecutive days. One- and two-day requests need 48 hours of notice when possible. Your manager must approve the request before you take the time. If two people on the same team request the same week, the earlier submission wins.

Unused PTO does not roll over after December 31. Employees who join mid-year receive a prorated bank: remaining months divided by 12, times 20 days, rounded up to the next half day. On your last day, unused PTO is paid out in the final paycheck at your current base rate.

## Sick leave
ACME provides 10 paid sick days per year, separate from PTO. Use sick leave for your own illness, a medical appointment, or to care for an immediate family member. You do not need a doctor's note for absences under three days. For three or more consecutive sick days, email a note to hr@acme.example within one week of returning.

Sick leave does not pay out on exit and does not roll over. If you exhaust sick leave, remaining days come from PTO with manager approval. Do not come to the office with a fever or a confirmed contagious illness; work from home if you feel able, otherwise take sick leave.

## Company holidays
ACME observes 11 paid holidays: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, the Friday after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, and Christmas Day. If a holiday falls on Saturday it is observed on Friday; if it falls on Sunday it is observed on Monday.

Engineering on-call coverage still applies on holidays. The on-call engineer is paid 1.5x hourly equivalent for any incident work, in addition to the holiday. Holiday work does not consume PTO.

## Parental leave
Birth parents and adoptive parents receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave, available within 12 months of the child's arrival. The leave may be taken as a single block or as two blocks of at least four weeks. Notify HR and your manager at least 30 days before the planned start date, or as soon as practicable for an adoption or medical situation.

Parental leave does not reduce your PTO or sick banks. Health insurance continues on the same contribution during leave. Before you return, schedule a 30-minute catch-up with your manager to plan workload and any temporary part-time ramp.
